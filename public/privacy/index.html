Privacy

Last updated November 2026

Local first

Deckard runs entirely in your browser. Your sets, presets, MIDI mappings and audio settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We don't have an account database and we don't store your projects on a server. Export a .deck file whenever you want a copy; clearing your browser data removes the rest.

No analytics

We don't run analytics, telemetry or ad trackers, and Deckard doesn't set cookies. Your audio and microphone or MIDI input are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Sign-in with Hypery

Signing in is optional and only needed for AI co-DJs and identity in shared rooms. Sign-in happens on Hypery (hypery.ai). Deckard asks for basic profile access and permission to use AI on your behalf. The resulting tokens and your basic profile (such as your name) are kept in your browser's local storage, and you can sign out at any time. Hypery's own terms and privacy policy cover your Hypery account.

AI co-DJs

When you add an AI co-DJ, the prompts it needs (the current set as .deck text and your instructions) are sent through Hypery to the AI model provider, and usage is billed to your Hypery account. Don't put anything in a set you wouldn't want an AI provider to process.

Multiplayer rooms

If you join a Co-DJ room, the edits you make in that room, your display name and room ID pass through our WebSocket gateway to the other people in it. If you're signed in, the gateway checks your Hypery token with Hypery to confirm who you are. Rooms live in memory only and are gone when everyone leaves; we don't save their contents. Like any server, the gateway may keep short-lived operational logs (such as connections and errors).

This website

deckard.lol is a static site served by DigitalOcean. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when they load. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs.
